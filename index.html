About me

I started in biochemical engineering, then realized I wanted to build things, not sit in a lab. That took me to cybersecurity and software, where I get to pick up an ambiguous problem and push it all the way to something that works. I am looking for a Solutions or Product role at a growth-stage, AI-native security company where I can stay close to customer problems and turn them into software that ships.
